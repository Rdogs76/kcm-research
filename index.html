Important: KCM Research is a screening tool built by a King's Capital Management student analyst for club discussion. The club is not a registered adviser, and nothing here is investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Labels ("Screens cheap", "Value-trap risk", "Thesis match") and percentiles rank stocks against each other within a ~140-stock universe; they are relative, automated, and can be wrong. Analyst ratings and targets are third-party opinions. Model values (residual income, peer-implied multiples) depend on the assumptions shown. Members may hold positions in stocks shown. Verify everything against company filings before presenting.

Data: market data, analyst ratings and quarterly statements from Yahoo Finance (delayed up to 15 minutes; statements can lag a quarter). Headlines from Yahoo Finance and Google News, which carries Bloomberg, Reuters, BNN Bloomberg, the Globe and Mail and others; each links to the original article and tone/theme tags are automated. Yield curves from the U.S. Treasury and the Bank of Canada; policy rates, credit spreads and real yields from FRED. Dealer trading results entered by hand from earnings releases. Economic calendar from ForexFactory. Watchlist and notes are stored only in this browser.

Shortcuts: R refresh · T theme · / search · 1–7 tabs.
